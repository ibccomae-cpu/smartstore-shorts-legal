Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026 · App: smartstore-shorts

This Privacy Policy explains how the smartstore-shorts application (“the App”) handles information when you create and upload product short videos.

1. Information we process

  • Account credentials you enter: Naver / app unlock password are used for login flows and are not sold.
  • OAuth tokens: TikTok, YouTube, or Instagram tokens may be stored locally on your device to upload videos.
  • API keys: Optional AI keys may be stored in your browser or server environment file.
  • Product data: Store URLs, titles, prices, and images fetched to generate videos.
  • Generated media: Video files saved in the App’s local output folder.

2. How we use information

Data is used only to generate shorts, display previews, and upload to platforms you connect. We do not sell personal data.

3. Storage

The App is designed for local use. Tokens and files typically remain on the machine running the App. Browser-stored keys stay in that browser’s local storage.

4. Third parties

When you upload, content and captions are sent to TikTok (and other platforms you choose) under their privacy policies. Temporary hosting may be used for some Instagram uploads.

5. Your choices

You can disconnect platforms (logout), delete local token files, clear browser storage, and delete generated videos at any time.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app operator who provided you access.
